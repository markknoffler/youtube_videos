[excited] To test these theorems for ourselves, we built a live diagnostic experiment in PyTorch, and ran it on this laptop.

We pitted two competing architectures against each other on the defining capability of modern foundational models: In-Context Associative Recall.

In this benchmark, the network is presented with arbitrary key-value pairs and must dynamically retrieve the correct value when queried with a corresponding key later in the sequence.

The first contender is a Self-Organizing Dynamic Rewiring Network, equipped with continuous Hebbian updates, localized synaptic competition, and top-k structural edge pruning.

The second contender is a small two-layer attention Transformer, trained with ordinary backpropagation.

Both models saw identical data: sixty-four dimensional embeddings, sixteen key-value pairs per sequence, thirteen possible values, for one hundred epochs.

[dramatic] The results were unequivocal.

[serious] For the first forty epochs, nothing seemed to happen. Both networks hovered just above chance, which for thirteen possible values is seven point seven percent.

The self-organizing network never left that floor. Its recall accuracy flatlined near twelve percent, completely unable to bind dynamic keys to their values in context.

Its local updates kept compressing its representation anyway. Its effective rank drifted down from forty-nine toward thirty, without ever learning the task.

And the gradient paths explain why. Structural mutations are discrete. A pruning mask is a step function, with no useful derivative. The optimization could not send credit across the links it was busy creating and destroying, locking the architecture into poor local minima.

[excited] The Transformer, meanwhile, did something remarkable. Around epoch forty-five, its accuracy suddenly jumped, from under thirty percent to ninety-nine percent in just four epochs, and then settled at one hundred.

That sudden leap looks exactly like the phase change Olsson and colleagues tied to the formation of induction heads.

[excited] The theory survived contact with real silicon. Without all-to-all bilinear attention, local rewiring algorithms cannot sustain dynamic in-context memory. The Transformer's dense matrix multiplications win decisively.
