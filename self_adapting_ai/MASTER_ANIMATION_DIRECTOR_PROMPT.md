### 1. THE NON-NEGOTIABLE VISUAL DIRECTIVE

The video **MUST NOT** behave like a slideshow with motion added on top. It **MUST** behave like a continuously evolving three-dimensional mathematical blackboard in which the explanation itself causes the geometry, particles, equations, camera, lighting, labels, graphs, and relationships to change.

The narration is the master clock. Every meaningful noun, verb, relationship, comparison, mathematical operation, causal claim, contrast, and conclusion must produce a corresponding visual state transition. The animation is not decoration beneath the narration. The animation is the visual execution of the narration.

A viewer should be able to pause at almost any point and answer: *What exact sentence is being spoken, what exact concept is being explained, and what exact visual operation is demonstrating it?* If the answer is merely “a 3D model is rotating while the narrator talks,” the shot has failed.

### 2. ABSOLUTE RULES FOR THE GENERATOR

1. **No static explanatory shot**: No asset may simply remain on screen while the narration changes topics. A persistent object may remain, but its state, camera relationship, illumination, connections, internal activity, labels, or geometry must evolve.
2. **No decorative motion**: Rotation, particles, glow, camera drift, and floating cubes are permitted only when they communicate structure, flow, hierarchy, time, computation, uncertainty, or emphasis.
3. **Narration controls motion**: Use word/syllable/phrase timestamps from the actual narration. Do not divide the video into arbitrary animation blocks independent of speech.
4. **Every sentence has a visual verb**: Examples: split, route, project, multiply, compare, freeze, prune, sprout, collapse, expand, converge, diverge, query, retrieve, attenuate, compress, branch, lock, unlock, transform.
5. **Every new concept changes state**: A new concept must introduce, transform, connect, remove, or reconfigure something. Merely changing text is insufficient.
6. **Continuous causality**: Transitions must be motivated. If the narrator says “project,” tokens physically travel through a projection plane. If the narrator says “freeze,” motion decelerates and a freeze front propagates through the parameter lattice.
7. **Spatial hierarchy**: The screen must have a primary explanatory object, secondary supporting objects, and tertiary contextual effects. Do not fill the frame randomly.
8. **Build before explaining**: When a complex object is introduced, construct it progressively: points → edges → labels → equations → active computation → result.
9. **Destroy when disproving**: When the narration exposes a limitation, visibly dismantle or constrain the previous mechanism rather than simply displaying a new card.
10. **Preserve continuity**: Objects representing the same concept should morph or travel into later representations whenever possible instead of disappearing and being replaced by unrelated assets.
11. **Camera is pedagogical**: Camera moves must reveal relationships: orbit to expose depth, dolly to magnify a mechanism, pan to follow a signal, pull back to reveal system-level structure, split-screen to compare systems.
12. **Equations are live objects**: Equations must be assembled term by term, with the exact visual operation corresponding to each mathematical term.
13. **Graphs are causally animated**: A plotted curve must be drawn as the underlying process occurs. Bars must rise/fall because a visible mechanism causes the change.
14. **Paper/code are evidence, not wallpaper**: When a paper or terminal appears, the camera must guide attention to the exact passage/line being discussed and then return the information into the 3D explanatory world.
15. **No arbitrary asset carousel**: Do not rotate through logos, papers, portraits, charts, and cubes merely to create variety. Each asset enters because the narration needs it.
16. **Dense but readable**: Populate the frame with interacting explanatory elements, but maintain one clear focal point at every moment.
17. **Motion never equals noise**: High animation density means many meaningful state changes, not chaotic movement.
18. **End every micro-explanation with a visible consequence**: A concept must produce a result: a route succeeds, a mode dominates, a graph stretches, a cache stalls, a rank collapses, or four technologies converge.

### 3. THE REQUIRED 1-SECOND ANIMATION ENGINE

The renderer must internally treat the 25-minute video as a 1-second choreography grid: 1,500 consecutive one-second windows at 30 FPS. Every second has an explicitly defined visual state, active objects, motion, cause, camera, lighting, text/equation state, and transition into the next second.

Within every one-second window, evaluate five layers:
- **Narrative layer**: exact words/phrase being spoken.
- **Semantic layer**: concept represented by those words.
- **Physical layer**: which object changes position, topology, geometry, intensity, scale, or state.
- **Attention layer**: what the camera and lighting force the viewer to notice.
- **Continuity layer**: what survives from the previous second and what must be introduced for the next.

#### Sub-second Choreography Rhythm
- `0.00–0.15 s`: **anticipation** — object begins responding.
- `0.15–0.40 s`: **primary action** — route/project/split/expand/collapse.
- `0.40–0.70 s`: **propagation** — consequence travels through connected objects.
- `0.70–0.90 s`: **emphasis** — relevant node/equation/region glows or enlarges.
- `0.90–1.00 s`: **settle/bridge** — state stabilises while the next action begins.

### 4. MOTION VOCABULARY — WHAT EACH VERB MUST LOOK LIKE

- **PROJECT**: Tokens leave the source space, pass through a visible projection plane/matrix, and emerge as a new coordinate representation. The projection plane illuminates the active dimensions.
- **ROUTE**: A signal/particle travels along the exact edge or attention connection being described. The path illuminates progressively behind the particle.
- **QUERY**: The querying token emits a controlled scan/ray toward candidate keys. Candidate key vectors briefly expose their coordinates before affinity is computed.
- **MULTIPLY**: Two visible vector/matrix structures approach, align, and generate a third structure.
- **WEIGHT**: Candidate paths change opacity/thickness/intensity according to the computed score.
- **FREEZE**: A visible shockwave travels through the parameter lattice. Motion stops behind the front. Locks appear only as a consequence of the freeze.
- **PRUNE**: An edge dims, contracts, disconnects, and physically retracts into the node rather than simply vanishing.
- **SPROUT**: A new edge grows from a node, first as a thin filament and then as a stable connection.
- **COLLAPSE**: Multiple independent modes visibly converge toward one axis/attractor. Spectral bars fall while one bar dominates.
- **EXPAND**: A compact representation unfolds into multiple dimensions, nodes, modes, experts, or branches.
- **COMPARE**: Two systems share a common visual coordinate frame. The same input enters both, revealing contrasting internal behaviors.
- **ATTENUATE**: A travelling signal loses intensity at every hop; the viewer sees the decay accumulate.
- **COMPRESS**: Many vectors/streams converge into a smaller latent representation, with information-preserving relationships remaining visible.
- **RETRIEVE**: The query highlights the matching key, then a signal follows the key-value binding to the exact value.
- **CONVERGE**: Separate technologies physically move toward a common central architecture and connect through visible interfaces.
- **DIVERGE**: One object splits into multiple distinct trajectories, each representing a different mode, expert, or hypothesis.
- **LOCK**: A component's moving internal state becomes stationary; a physical latch/constraint closes and associated telemetry changes to zero.
- **SEARCH**: A controlled scanning field moves across candidates; false candidates dim and the selected candidate remains active.
- **EMERGE**: Small local interactions accumulate into a higher-level structure.

### 5. CAMERA, LIGHTING, DEPTH, AND COMPOSITION CONTRACT

- **3D Scene Continuity**: Equations and papers exist as embedded spatial surfaces within the 3D volume.
- **Camera Discipline**: Camera motion corresponds strictly to conceptual scale (orbit to establish, dolly to enter mechanism, push to inspect math, track to follow signal, pull back to reveal consequence).
- **Lighting Hierarchy**: Active explanatory object has highest luminance; causal paths carry emissive pulses; dead/pruned paths desaturate and dim.
- **Depth Layers**: Foreground (active mechanism) -> Midground (supporting context/evidence) -> Background (environment, telemetry, latent architecture).
